Version: 1.0
Status: Draft

# Executive Summary

Titan AI Trading System is an institutional-grade, AI-driven, multi-agent trading platform designed to analyze financial markets, reason about trading opportunities, manage risk, and execute trades through a modular and scalable architecture.

The system is intended to evolve over multiple years without requiring major architectural redesigns. Every component must remain independent, testable, maintainable, and extensible.

The platform will support Stocks, Forex, Cryptocurrencies, ETFs, Indices, Commodities, and any future asset class through a plug-in architecture.

# Core Principles

## Explainability

Every trading decision must include a clear explanation.

No black-box decisions.

---

## Modularity

Every component must be replaceable without affecting the rest of the system.

---

## Scalability

The architecture must support adding new Agents, Markets, Strategies, Brokers, and AI Models without major redesign.

---

## Reliability

Every action must be logged.

Every error must be traceable.

Every important event must be recoverable.

---

## Security

Security is mandatory.

Secrets must never be hardcoded.

Sensitive data must always be protected.

---

## Performance

Optimize only after correctness.

Correctness is always the highest priority.

---

## Testability

Every module should be independently testable.

Every critical feature should include automated tests.

---

## Maintainability

The codebase should remain understandable even after several years of development.
