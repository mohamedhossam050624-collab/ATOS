# Architectural Domains

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines the business domains of the Artificial Trading Operating System (ATOS).

Each domain owns a specific business responsibility and must remain independent. Communication between domains should occur through well-defined interfaces and events rather than direct dependencies whenever possible.

This separation enables scalability, maintainability, extensibility, and fault isolation.

---

# Domain Philosophy

Every domain has one clear responsibility.

A domain should never perform responsibilities that belong to another domain.

Domains may collaborate, but they must remain logically independent.

# Domain Interaction Rules

- Every domain owns its own business logic.
- No domain should directly modify another domain's internal state.
- Communication should occur through services, events, or well-defined interfaces.
- Dependencies must remain minimal.
- Each domain should be independently testable.

---

# Summary

The domain architecture establishes clear ownership boundaries across the platform.

Every future component, service, AI agent, workflow, plugin, and feature must belong to exactly one primary domain.
