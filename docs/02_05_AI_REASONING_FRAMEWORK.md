# AI Reasoning Framework

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines how AI components inside ATOS reason before producing any trading recommendation.

The goal is not to predict the future with certainty.

The goal is to make the highest quality decision possible using available evidence while explicitly recognizing uncertainty.

---

# Philosophy

ATOS does not assume it is correct.

ATOS assumes it may be wrong.

Every conclusion must survive critical evaluation before becoming a recommendation.

Reasoning always comes before decision.

---

# The Scientific Method

The reasoning process follows a scientific approach.

1. Observe
2. Form a Hypothesis
3. Collect Evidence
4. Validate Evidence
5. Search for Contradictions
6. Generate Alternative Explanations
7. Estimate Confidence
8. Evaluate Risk
9. Produce Recommendation
10. Review Outcome

# Hypothesis Generation

The system begins by generating one or more hypotheses.

Examples:

- Bullish continuation
- Bearish reversal
- Range-bound movement
- High volatility expansion
- False breakout

Multiple hypotheses may exist simultaneously.

---

# Evidence Collection

Evidence may come from:

- Technical Analysis
- Market Structure
- Liquidity Analysis
- Volume Profile
- Order Flow
- Macro Events
- News
- Sentiment
- Fundamentals
- Correlation Analysis

Every evidence source must be traceable.

---

# Evidence Validation

Before evidence is accepted, the system verifies:

- Source reliability
- Data freshness
- Data completeness
- Internal consistency

Invalid evidence must be discarded.

---

# Contradiction Analysis

The system actively searches for evidence against each hypothesis.

Examples:

Bullish trend

Contradicting evidence:

- Strong resistance
- Negative news
- Weak volume
- Bearish macro conditions

A hypothesis that survives contradiction becomes stronger.

---

# Alternative Scenario Analysis

The system generates alternative explanations.

Example:

Instead of assuming:

"This is a breakout."

It also evaluates:

- False breakout
- Liquidity grab
- Distribution
- Market manipulation

The recommendation should consider competing explanations.

---

# Confidence Estimation

Confidence is determined by:

- Evidence quality
- Evidence agreement
- Historical reliability
- Data completeness
- Market conditions

Confidence must never be based on intuition alone.

---

# Risk Evaluation

Every hypothesis is evaluated for:

- Downside risk
- Expected reward
- Market uncertainty
- Volatility
- Portfolio exposure

A strong hypothesis may still be rejected if risk is unacceptable.

---

# Recommendation

Possible outputs:

- Buy
- Sell
- Hold
- Wait
- Reanalyze

Every recommendation must include:

- Supporting evidence
- Contradicting evidence
- Confidence
- Risk assessment
- Explanation

---

# Learning Loop

After every completed trade, the system evaluates:

- Was the hypothesis correct?
- Which evidence was useful?
- Which evidence was misleading?
- Were contradictions ignored?
- What should improve next time?

The goal is continuous improvement.

---

# Design Rules

The reasoning engine must never:

- Ignore conflicting evidence.
- Hide uncertainty.
- Recommend trades without evidence.
- Override risk management.
- Fabricate explanations.

---

# Success Criteria

The framework is successful if:

- Decisions are explainable.
- Evidence is traceable.
- Alternative scenarios are considered.
- Contradictions are analyzed.
- Learning improves future reasoning.

---

# Summary

The AI Reasoning Framework ensures that ATOS thinks critically before acting.

The objective is not certainty.

The objective is disciplined, evidence-based decision making under uncertainty.
